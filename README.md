# FINOTE TSIDIK — Product Feature Overview

A comprehensive summary of the platform's capabilities, organized by functional area for quick onboarding.

---

## 1. Membership Management

The core module for maintaining the organization's member directory across all life stages and group structures.

- **Member Profiles**
  - Capture full personal, contact, emergency, and spiritual information
  - Track parents/guardians and family relationships
  - Maintain employment, education, and occupational history
  - Store a complete status history timeline for each member

- **Membership Lifecycle**
  - Status workflow: Draft → Member → Active → Former
  - Soft deletion with restore capability
  - Group assignments and department affiliations

- **Groups & Classifications**
  - Categorize members into groups (e.g., Kids, Youth, Adults)
  - Bulk assignment and removal from groups
  - Department-level scoping for distributed teams

- **Parent & Guardian Linking**
  - Link children to parent/guardian records
  - View all children from a parent's profile
  - Bulk sync and validation of family relationships

---

## 2. Education Management

Manages the organization's education programs including classes, teachers, students, and academic years.

- **Academic Year Management**
  - Define active and past academic years
  - Control enrollment eligibility by year

- **Student Enrollment**
  - Enroll members into classes under an active academic year
  - Status tracking: Enrolled, Withdrawn, Completed, Promoted
  - Bulk promotion to the next academic year with duplicate prevention
  - Undo promotion and cancel enrollment actions

- **Teacher Management**
  - Maintain teacher profiles (internal members or external hires)
  - Track qualifications and active class assignments
  - Status control: Active, Inactive, On Leave, Former
  - Attendance rate display for the active academic year

- **Classroom & Subject Setup**
  - Define classrooms, subjects, and course structures
  - Build course catalogs with categories and lessons

- **Attendance Tracking**
  - Mark student attendance by class and session
  - statuses: Present, Absent, Late, Permission, Excused
  - Bulk status application and summary reporting
  - Teacher attendance tracking with dedicated reports

- **Performance Reports**
  - Student progress reports
  - Class performance summaries
  - Teacher attendance reports

---

## 3. Contributions & Banking

Handles recurring member contributions, donation tracking, and payment validation.

- **Contribution Records**
  - Record payments tied to academic years and months
  - Auto-fill expected contribution amounts based on group rules
  - Payment method and paid-status tracking
  - Archive protection for historical contributions

- **Contribution Reports**
  - Filter by academic year, member group, and date range
  - Top contributors leaderboard
  - Export reports in Excel or CSV format

- **Donation Management**
  - Track one-time and recurring donations
  - Donor classification and receipt tracking
  - Linked to fundraising campaigns

- **Fundraising Campaigns**
  - Create and manage targeted fundraising drives
  - Track progress toward goals

---

## 4. Financial Management

The organization's full bookkeeping and financial oversight module.

- **Income & Expense Tracking**
  - Categorized line items (tithes, offerings, donations, salaries, utilities, maintenance, etc.)
  - Multi-currency support
  - Receipt and invoice attachments
  - Automatic transaction ID generation

- **Financial Reports**
  - **Financial Overview**: Real-time summary of income, expenses, net position, contributions, donations, and bank balances
  - **Financial Statements**: Period-based statements with drill-down capability
  - **Financial Analytics**: Trend analysis and breakdown charts
  - **Audit Trail**: Complete change history for financial records

- **Bank Account Management**
  - Track multiple organizational bank accounts
  - Monitor current balances per account
  - Link transactions to specific accounts

- **Approval Workflows**
  - Pending approval queues for financial transactions
  - Role-based approval permissions

---

## 5. Charity & Aid Management

Manages charitable programs, beneficiaries, and aid distribution.

- **Beneficiary Registry**
  - Profile management for individuals, families, and organizations
  - Auto-generated beneficiary codes
  - Need categories: Food, Medical, Education, Housing
  - Status tracking: Active, Inactive, Completed

- **Aid Distribution**
  - Record aid distributions linked to beneficiaries
  - Track total aid received and last distribution date per beneficiary
  - Conditional safeguards (cannot delete beneficiaries with distribution history)

- **Charity Reports**
  - Summaries of active beneficiaries and aid distributed
  - Breakdowns by beneficiary type and status

---

## 6. Inventory Management

Tracks physical assets, stock movements, and alerts.

- **Item Catalog**
  - Categorize items (Electronics, Furniture, Books, Supplies, Equipment)
  - Purchase details and condition tracking
  - Status management: Active, Damaged, Lost, Disposed

- **Stock Control**
  - Real-time stock level calculation based on movements
  - Low-stock highlighting and alerts
  - Stock-in and stock-out history per item

- **Inventory Actions**
  - Bulk mark items as damaged
  - Low-stock filtering and reporting

---

## 7. Tour Management

Organizes trips, registrations, and attendance for group travel.

- **Tour Planning**
  - Define destination, date range, cost per person, and capacity
  - Registration deadlines and passenger limits
  - Status workflow: Draft → Published → In Progress → Completed / Cancelled

- **Passenger Registration**
  - Member and non-member passenger signup
  - Capacity enforcement with waitlist protection
  - Confirmed passenger count tracking

- **Tour Attendance**
  - Dedicated attendance sessions per tour
  - Track who participated vs. who only registered

- **Tour Reporting**
  - Export tour manifests and financial summaries
  - Status breakdown charts

---

## 8. Events & Rehearsals

Schedules and manages organizational events and music/choir rehearsals.

- **Event Management**
  - Schedule events with location, date/time, images, and descriptions
  - Optional registration with capacity limits and deadlines
  - Recurring events (weekly, monthly, custom)
  - Status workflow: Draft → Published → Full → Ongoing → Completed → Cancelled

- **Rehearsal Scheduling**
  - Plan rehearsals with time, location, and linked songs
  - Recurring session support
  - Attendance tracking (present vs. total)
  - Bulk mark as Completed or Cancelled

---

## 9. Content & Media Management

Manages public-facing and internal content including media, blog posts, and documents.

- **Media Library**
  - Upload and organize photos and videos
  - Bulk upload support
  - Category/subcategory classification and event albums
  - Visibility control (Public / Hidden)
  - Image editing with preset aspect ratios

- **Blog Posts**
  - Bilingual content creation (English and Amharic)
  - Scheduled publishing with date picker
  - Featured post highlighting and tags
  - Status workflow: Draft → Scheduled → Published → Archived
  - Bulk publish and archive actions

- **Documents & Resources**
  - Centralized document storage and categorization
  - Library resources with subcategories
  - Download and view tracking

- **Announcements & FAQs**
  - Publish announcements to the organization
  - Maintain a searchable FAQ knowledge base

- **Pages**
  - Manage static website pages
  - SEO-friendly URLs and publishing control

---

## 10. User Management & Security

Controls who can access the system and what they can do.

- **User Accounts**
  - Create and manage system users and administrators
  - Role-based access control with granular permissions
  - Department assignment
  - Language preference (English / Amharic)

- **Account Security**
  - Account lock/unlock with reason logging
  - Force password reset to temporary passwords
  - Force logout (terminate all active sessions)
  - Self-protection rules prevent accidental self-locking or deletion
  - Bulk lock/unlock capability

- **Session Management**
  - View and manage active user sessions
  - Session timeout controls
  - Track failed login attempts

---

## 11. Attendance System

A unified attendance engine for students, teachers, tours, and rehearsals.

- **Attendance Sessions**
  - Create sessions linked to classes, tours, or rehearsals
  - Open/closed session status control
  - Auto-locking capability for historical integrity

- **Marking Attendance**
  - Present, Absent, Late, Permission, Excused statuses
  - Bulk marking and select-all shortcuts
  - Real-time summary display while marking

- **Offline Sync**
  - Record attendance offline and sync later
  - Conflict detection and resolution

---

## 12. System Administration

Tools for maintaining platform health, settings, and governance.

- **Global Settings**
  - Configure organization name, address, phone, and email
  - Upload and manage the organization logo
  - Default language and maintenance mode controls

- **System Health Monitoring**
  - Real-time health metrics dashboard
  - Critical, warning, and healthy status indicators
  - Automated refresh and reporting

- **Audit Logging**
  - Read-only system-wide activity trail
  - Security and financial tier classifications
  - User attribution, IP tracking, and before/after change values

- **Backup & Restore**
  - System backup generation
  - Restore from backup capability

- **Custom Options**
  - Manage dropdown values and configurable lists across the platform

- **User Manual**
  - Built-in help documentation for end users

---

## 13. Reporting & Analytics

Role-based dashboards and exportable reports for informed decision-making.

- **Role-Based Dashboards**
  - Each role sees widgets relevant to their responsibilities:
    - Leadership sees membership and financial summaries
    - Finance sees income, expenses, and net position
    - Education sees enrollment and attendance trends
    - Charity sees beneficiary and aid metrics
    - Inventory sees stock levels and movements
    - Content sees published media and blog posts

- **Charts & Visualizations**
  - Revenue and expense trends
  - Member demographics (gender, type, group distribution)
  - Enrollment and attendance trends
  - Inventory by category
  - Tour status and beneficiary breakdowns

- **Export Capabilities**
  - Export lists and reports to Excel, CSV, or PDF
  - Background export processing with notifications when ready
  - Configurable column selection

---

## 14. Communication & Notifications

Keeps members and staff informed.

- **Announcement Broadcasting**
  - Send announcements to specific groups or organization-wide
  - Track read receipts where applicable

- ** Automated Notifications**
  - Rehearsal reminders
  - Contribution and donation acknowledgments
  - Event registration confirmations
  - Schedule-based outstanding contribution notices

- **Contact Messages**
  - Manage inbound inquiries and messages from the public

---

## 15. Mobile & Accessibility

- **Progressive Web App**
  - Installable on mobile devices and desktops
  - Offline capability for key functions such as attendance
  - Optimized for both English and Amharic with Noto Sans Ethiopic font support
